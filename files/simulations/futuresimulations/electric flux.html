Area Flux

Imagine electric flux as counting how many "electric field lines" pierce through a given area.

Formula
Φ = E · A · cos(θ)

E: Electric Field Strength

A: Surface Area

θ: Angle between Field and Surface Normal

Gauss's Law

The total electric flux out of a closed surface is determined entirely by the charge enclosed within it.

Formula
ΦE = ∮ E · dA = Q / ε0

Q: Enclosed Charge

Surface: Changing the sphere's radius alters the surface area, but the total flux remains constant!
